# 0006 What the website is for, and who sends the newsletter

- Question: what does the Zur Einsicht website do, and who is the sender?
- Options: an archive of the editions; a home for the blog posts; a shop window for courses and bitz GmbH; my personal home. As sender: me, bitz GmbH, or Zur Einsicht as a brand of its own.
- Choice: the website holds the archive of the editions and the blog posts. My personal home stays on lse.de. I am the sender, in my own name and voice, and bitz GmbH is the publisher in the imprint.
- Why: a publication is smaller than an identity, so the website does not hold up the first edition. The newsletter is a person's voice. The company publishes it, and the website links out to my courses and to bitz GmbH.
