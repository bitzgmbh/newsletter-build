# Edition template

Draft for feedback at the retreat, 2026-10-01. The parts and word counts are a proposal for an edition of 1,000 words or fewer.

| Part | What goes in | Words |
|---|---|---|
| Subject line and opening | Written by me, in my voice | 80 |
| The idea | One idea per edition, in small parts with bullets | 450 |
| Example or story | From my own work and teaching | 200 |
| Try it yourself | One step that starts from a task readers do in Excel today and uses their own knowledge. R in the browser and an AI prompt alternate from edition to edition (A4 in [concept.md](concept.md)) | 100 |
| One question for the reader | Answered with one click or a reply | 40 |
| What readers said last time | The answers to the previous question | 50 |
| How this edition was made | The AI disclosure, as bullets | 60 |
| Pointers | Blog post, course, other channels, and the line for forwarded readers ("Weitergeleitet bekommen? Hier abonnieren") with its own link | 20 |
| **Total** | | **1,000** |

## Newsletter and blog

One blog post per four editions, about every two months, presented in the edition that follows. Two options are open:

- A round-up: all four editions feed one post, each topic in more detail.
- A deep dive: the edition that drew the most answers becomes one post, in depth. This is the current suggestion.
